Growing tomatoes in pots

Tomatoes want at least six hours of direct sun a day. A pot of 20 litres or more keeps the roots cool and holds enough water to get through a hot afternoon.

Water deeply and evenly. Letting the soil swing between bone dry and soaked is the usual cause of split fruit and of blossom end rot, the dark sunken patch at the bottom of the tomato.

Pinch out the side shoots of tall (cordon) varieties so the plant puts its energy into fruit, and tie the main stem to a stake as it grows.
