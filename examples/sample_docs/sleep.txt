Sleeping better

A fixed wake-up time, kept on weekends too, does more for sleep than a fixed bedtime. The body clock anchors on morning light, so a few minutes outside soon after waking helps.

Caffeine has a half-life of around five hours, so an afternoon coffee is still partly active at midnight. A cool, dark and quiet bedroom makes falling asleep easier.

If you lie awake for a long time, getting up and doing something calm in dim light until you feel sleepy works better than staying in bed and trying harder.
