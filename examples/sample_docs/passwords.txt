Choosing passwords

Length matters more than clever symbols. A passphrase of four or five random words is easier to remember and harder to guess than a short password full of substitutions.

Never reuse a password across sites. When one site leaks its database, attackers try the same email and password everywhere else. A password manager makes unique passwords practical.

Turn on two-factor authentication for email and banking first: whoever controls your email can reset the passwords of almost every other account you own.
