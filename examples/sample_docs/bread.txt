Keeping bread fresh

Bread goes stale mostly because its starch recrystallises, not because it dries out. That process is fastest at fridge temperature, so a loaf kept in the refrigerator turns stale sooner than one left on the counter.

For a loaf you will finish in two or three days, a paper bag or a bread box on the counter is best. Plastic keeps the crumb soft but makes a crisp crust go leathery.

For longer storage, slice the loaf and freeze it in a sealed bag. Slices toast straight from frozen, and a whole frozen loaf revives well after ten minutes in a hot oven.
