Bicycle chain care

A chain that squeaks or looks orange needs attention. Wipe it with a rag while turning the pedals backwards, then put one drop of chain lubricant on each roller.

Let the lube soak in for a few minutes and wipe off the excess. Oil left on the outside of the chain only collects grit, which grinds down the chain and the sprockets.

Chains stretch as the pins wear. A cheap chain checker tells you when to replace it; changing a worn chain early is much cheaper than replacing the cassette and chainrings it would otherwise wear out.
